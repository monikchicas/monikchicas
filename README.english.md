### 📌 Featured Projects

**[Correlation Analysis and Executive Dashboard — Global Electronics Retailer](https://github.com/monikchicas/Analisis-de-Correlacion-y-Dashboard-Ejecutivo-Global-Electronics-Retailer)**

Correlation and linear regression analysis applied to a multinational
retail electronics chain operating in 8 countries, identifying which
factors relate to store and customer performance. Includes customer
segmentation by spend quartile (the top 25% of customers generates
62.7% of total revenue) and an interactive 3-page executive dashboard
in Power BI.

`SQL Server` `Power BI` `DAX` `Correlation & Regression` `Window Functions`

**[Delivery Time Analysis — DataCo Global Supply Chain](https://github.com/monikchicas/Analisis-de-Cadena-de-suministro---supply-chain-analysis)**

Analysis of delivery punctuality and its economic impact on customer
retention, using SQL Server to answer 5 business questions and Power
BI + DAX for an interactive dashboard. Includes full documentation
of the process and methodological decisions.

`SQL Server` `Power BI` `DAX` `Business Analysis`
